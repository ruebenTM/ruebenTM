Why it matters

Adolescence is one of the most important times for your body's development. What you eat now shapes how you feel, think, and grow for decades.

🧠

Brain Power

Good nutrition fuels concentration, memory, and learning — essential for school performance every day.

💪

Growth & Strength

Protein, calcium, and iron support rapid muscle and bone development throughout your teenage years.

⚡

Energy All Day

Complex carbohydrates provide slow-release energy to keep you alert and focused all day.

🛡️

Disease Prevention

A balanced diet rich in vitamins, minerals, and fibre lowers your risk of chronic disease later in life.

Every food group plays a unique role. Adolescents need serves from all five every single day to stay healthy and strong.

Grains give your body energy to get through the day. They're packed with fibre, B vitamins, iron, and magnesium. Always try to pick wholegrain — it's way better for you.

💪 High Protein

Caesar Chicken Wrap

⏱ 10 mins🍽 1 serve🥗 Classic

You Need

  • 1 large tortilla wrap
  • 1 cup cooked chicken (shredded or chopped)
  • 1 handful crunchy lettuce
  • 1–2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 1 tbsp grated parmesan
  • Optional: a few croutons (add right before eating)

How to Make It

  1. Mix the chicken with the Caesar dressing — just enough to coat it.
  2. Lay the tortilla flat and add the lettuce first (stops the wrap getting soggy).
  3. Add the chicken mixture and sprinkle parmesan on top.
  4. Roll it up tightly, fold the ends in, and slice in half.
  5. Wrap in foil or baking paper so it stays together in your lunchbox.
Chicken is a lean protein that supports muscle growth. Lettuce and parmesan add vitamins and calcium.

🌱 Vegetarian

Cream Cheese & Veggie Wrap

⏱ 8 mins🍽 1 serve🥦 Fresh

You Need

  • 1 large tortilla wrap
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese (plain or herb)
  • ¼ cucumber, cut into thin sticks
  • ½ carrot, grated or cut into thin sticks
  • A handful of lettuce or spinach
  • A pinch of salt & pepper
  • Optional: a drizzle of sweet chilli sauce

How to Make It

  1. Lay the tortilla flat on a clean surface.
  2. Spread the cream cheese all over the wrap — right to the edges.
  3. Place the cucumber, carrot, and lettuce in a line down the middle.
  4. Add a tiny pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Add a small drizzle of sweet chilli sauce if you like.
  6. Roll the wrap tightly, folding the sides in as you go.
  7. Slice it in half and wrap it in foil or baking paper.
Carrots are rich in vitamin A and cucumber adds hydration. Cream cheese provides calcium for strong bones.

Simple Veggie Pasta Salad

⏱ 15 mins🍽 1–2 serves🎒 Make Ahead

You Need

  • 1 cup cooked pasta (spirals or penne work best)
  • ½ cup chopped veggies (cucumber, cherry tomatoes, corn, capsicum)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt & pepper
  • Optional: a sprinkle of grated cheese

How to Make It

  1. Cook the pasta, drain it, and let it cool completely.
  2. Chop your veggies into small bite-sized pieces.
  3. In a small cup, mix olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. Pour the dressing over the pasta and veggies.
  5. Mix everything well so the pasta is lightly coated.
  6. Add cheese if you want. Pack in a container and keep it chilled.
Pasta provides carbohydrates for sustained energy. Colourful veggies add a range of vitamins and antioxidants.

🍗 Chicken

Cold Chicken Noodle Bowl

⏱ 10 mins🍽 1 serve🥢 Asian-Inspired

You Need

  • 1 cup cooked noodles (ramen, rice noodles, or spaghetti)
  • ½ cup shredded chicken
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp honey
  • A tiny splash of sesame oil (optional)
  • Cucumber slices or carrot sticks

How to Make It

  1. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil.
  2. Add the noodles and chicken.
  3. Toss everything until the noodles are lightly coated.
  4. Add cucumber or carrot for crunch.
  5. Pack in a container and keep chilled.
Chicken and noodles provide protein and carbohydrates. Honey gives natural sweetness while adding a touch of energy.

🌱 Vegetarian

Cucumber, Avocado & Feta Sandwich

⏱ 10 mins🍽 1 serve🥑 Super Fresh

You Need

  • 2 slices of bread (wholemeal or white)
  • ½ ripe avocado
  • ¼ cucumber, sliced thin
  • 2–3 tbsp crumbled feta cheese
  • A handful of spinach or lettuce
  • A tiny squeeze of lemon, salt & pepper
  • Optional: a drizzle of olive oil

How to Make It

  1. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  2. Spread the mashed avocado evenly over both slices of bread.
  3. Lay the thin cucumber slices on top of the avocado.
  4. Sprinkle the crumbled feta over the cucumber.
  5. Put a handful of spinach or lettuce on top and press down gently.
  6. Add a tiny drizzle of olive oil for extra richness.
  7. Put the second slice of bread on top. Cut and pack.
Avocado is packed with healthy fats for brain development. Feta provides calcium and protein. Perfect for warm days!

Egg & Veggie Mini Sandwiches

⏱ 15 mins🍽 1 serve💪 Protein-Packed

You Need

  • 2 slices of bread (white, wholemeal, or grain)
  • 1 hard-boiled egg
  • 1 tbsp mayo or Greek yogurt
  • A pinch of salt & pepper
  • ¼ carrot, grated
  • A few thin cucumber slices
  • Optional: a tiny sprinkle of paprika or chives

How to Make It

  1. Boil the egg for 10 minutes. Cool under cold water and peel.
  2. Mash the egg with mayo (or yogurt), salt, and pepper until creamy.
  3. Spread the egg mixture evenly on one slice of bread.
  4. Sprinkle the grated carrot over the egg mixture.
  5. Add thin cucumber slices on top for crunch.
  6. Place the second slice of bread on top and press gently.
  7. Cut into 4 small squares or 2 triangles. Pack in a container.
Eggs are a complete protein with all essential amino acids. Carrots and cucumber add vitamins and a satisfying crunch.

What is a recipe modification?

A modification is a simple ingredient swap or change that makes a recipe more nutritious. Below you'll see the original recipe next to the healthier version, with every change clearly highlighted.

🌯 Caesar Chicken Wrap

Swapping Caesar dressing for yogurt and adding more vegetables reduces fat and boosts nutrients.

Before

Classic Caesar Chicken Wrap

  • 1 large tortilla wrap
  • 1 cup cooked chicken
  • 1 handful lettuce
  • 2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 1 tbsp grated parmesan
  • Croutons
Method
  1. Mix chicken with Caesar dressing.
  2. Add lettuce to wrap.
  3. Add chicken, parmesan, croutons.
  4. Roll up and slice.
After — Healthier

Lighter Caesar Chicken Wrap

  • Wholemeal wrapModplain white tortilla
  • 1 cup cooked chicken
  • Lettuce + sliced cucumber + grated carrotModlettuce only
  • 1 tbsp Greek yogurt + 1 tsp lemon juiceMod2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 1 tbsp grated parmesan
  • Skip croutons or use only a fewMod
Why These Changes?
  1. Wholemeal wrap adds more fibre than white.
  2. Extra veggies add vitamins A and C.
  3. Greek yogurt has less fat than Caesar dressing and adds protein and calcium.
  4. Fewer croutons reduces refined carbs and sodium.

Australian Dietary Guidelines

Health Advice for Teens

Understanding what your body needs during adolescence — the most important period of growth in your life.

Evidence-based recommendations from the National Health and Medical Research Council (NHMRC).

1

Eat from all 5 food groups daily

Include vegetables, fruit, grain foods, lean meats/alternatives, and dairy every day. Variety ensures you get all essential vitamins and minerals your body needs to grow.

2

Water as your main drink

Teens should aim for 8–10 glasses of water daily. Avoid sugary drinks like soft drinks and energy drinks — they contribute to tooth decay and excess kilojoule intake.

3

Limit saturated fat, salt & sugar

Processed snack foods and fast food should be occasional treats. Excessive saturated fat and sugar is linked to obesity and cardiovascular disease.

4

Encourage breastfeeding

This guideline applies to infant nutrition but builds the foundation of healthy eating habits from birth — setting up lifelong healthy patterns for all Australians.

5

Prepare and store food safely

Safe food handling prevents foodborne illness. Always refrigerate perishables, wash hands before eating, and use clean utensils — especially important for packed lunchboxes!

6

Achieve a healthy weight

Balance kilojoule intake with at least 60 minutes of physical activity per day. This supports both physical and mental wellbeing throughout your entire adolescence.
